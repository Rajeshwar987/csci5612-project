Clustering

Grouping vehicles by their combined safety profile, rather than by manufacturer or body style.

This section is completed in Module 2. The structure below is in place and will be filled with the analysis, results, and visualisations for Clustering as the course reaches that module.

Overview

Partitional and hierarchical clustering will be applied to the joined model-year table to test whether vehicles fall into natural safety groupings, and whether those groupings correspond to anything a buyer would recognise.
