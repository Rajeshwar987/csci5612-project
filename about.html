About Me

I am a graduate student in Data Science with a background in Electronics and Communication Engineering and professional experience across software quality engineering and data engineering.

From 2022 to 2025 I worked as a Software Quality Engineer at Pegasystems, where I worked on enterprise software and developed experience with testing, automation, APIs, data, and large-scale business systems.

During the summer of 2026 I worked as a Data Engineer intern at Denver International Airport, where I worked with data and technology projects in an operational environment. This experience gave me an opportunity to apply my data and engineering skills to real-world airport systems.

My current machine learning project brings together my technical background with my long-standing interest in automobiles and vehicle safety. I am investigating a simple but interesting question:

Do the crash-test scores used to sell vehicles tell us anything useful about the problems their owners actually experience?

Rather than treating this as simply a machine learning modelling exercise, I am working through the full data science lifecycle — from defining the question and preparing the data to exploratory analysis, feature engineering, modelling, evaluation, and communicating the results.

My undergraduate background in electronics and communications initially sparked my interest in technology and real-world systems. My professional experience in software engineering and data engineering, combined with my graduate studies in Data Science, has given me a broader toolkit for investigating those systems through data.

Outside of academics and work, I enjoy automobiles, motorcycle riding, racing games, and listening to jazz.
